Our History

Founded in 1998 in beautiful Cache Valley, Utah, Logan River Rafting began with a simple passion — sharing the excitement of whitewater with friends and family. What started as weekend trips on the Logan River has grown into one of Northern Utah’s most trusted rafting companies.

Over 25 years later, we still operate with the same values: safety first, unforgettable experiences, and respect for the rivers we love.
