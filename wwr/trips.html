Trip Details

1. Family Scenic Float

Duration: 2.5 hours   |   Difficulty: Easy   |   Ages: 5+

$59 per person

Our most popular trip! Drift peacefully down the lower Logan River through beautiful meadows and red rock canyons. Spot deer, eagles, and maybe even a moose. Ideal for families, first-timers, and those who want to relax and enjoy the scenery. All equipment and professional guides included.

  • Life jackets and paddles provided
  • Photo stops at scenic viewpoints
  • Minimum 2 people per raft
Reserve This Trip

2. Whitewater Thrills Expedition

Duration: 3.5 hours   |   Difficulty: Moderate   |   Ages: 12+

$89 per person

Get your adrenaline pumping on the upper section of the Logan River! Navigate exciting Class III rapids with names like "The Wall" and "S-turn." Our experienced guides will keep you safe while maximizing the fun. Includes a riverside snack break with stunning mountain views.

  • Wetsuits available upon request
  • Helmets and high-quality gear
  • Post-trip refreshments
Reserve This Trip

3. Full Day Canyon Explorer

Duration: 6 hours   |   Difficulty: Moderate to Strenuous   |   Ages: 14+

$149 per person

The ultimate Logan River experience! Combine scenic floats and whitewater sections with a delicious riverside lunch. Explore hidden waterfalls and side canyons that most visitors never see. Perfect for adventure seekers who want a full day on the water.

  • Includes lunch and snacks
  • Multiple rafting sections
  • Guided nature interpretation
